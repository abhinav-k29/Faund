# Wilsee logo assets

The active logo is the original user-supplied bright green and purple sprout, with its original colours and transparent background. The canonical artwork is `public/brand-sprout.png`; header, browser/PWA icons and social metadata use content-fingerprinted copies under `public/brand/wilsee-original/` so a previously cached colour variant is not reused. The user asked to restore this version on 25 October 2026.

A muted colour experiment was made using the built-in imagegen tool and then withdrawn at the user’s request. It is preserved as an unused source asset below; it is not the active logo.

The withdrawn preview is archived at `assets/brand/wilsee-logo-muted-preview.png`, outside the published app. The old `/brand-sprout-muted.png` address redirects to the original logo. The full edited master is `assets/brand/wilsee-logo-muted-source.png`. Browser/PWA icons are exported at 192/512 px; the maskable and Apple versions use the app's cream background. Resizing preserves the generated alpha channel.

Palette: forest `#163e35`, mid green `#456c50`, sage `#91a873`, cream `#f7f5ed`, pale sage `#e4ebd6`, dusty lavender `#ddd5e9`.

## Archived experiment prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: Wilsee app logo, transparent PNG. Image 1 is the edit target: the supplied glossy two-leaf sprout emerging from two layered rounded diamond tiles, with two small rounded viewfinder corner marks. Image 2 is ONLY the existing app UI palette reference, not an edit target and must not appear in the output. Change only the logo's colours and saturation to fit the calm nature app. Preserve the exact original silhouette, two leaves, stem, two layered tiles, corner marks, composition, perspective, glossy dimensional shading and crisp alpha edges. Leaves and stem should use restrained forest green #163e35, mid green #456c50 and soft sage highlights #91a873; remove fluorescent lime/yellow-green highlights and highly saturated emerald. Leaf veins can be pale warm sage. Recolour the bright purple tiles to warm cream #f7f5ed, pale sage #e4ebd6 and subtle dusty lavender #ddd5e9; no electric violet or blue. Corner marks should be muted sage/forest green. Keep soft natural highlights, readable separation between layers and a polished small-icon appearance. Genuine transparent background everywhere outside the logo, including holes; no white/black background, no rectangle or checkerboard. No text, letters, watermark, new objects, UI, borders or redesign. Output one square logo on transparency.
