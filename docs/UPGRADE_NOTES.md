All six companions use real volumetric geometry. Pipwing has wings, tail, crest and a face; Bumbloom has abdominal bands, translucent wings, legs and antennae; Wattlet has botanical leaves and golden flower clusters. Bramblewisp has a slim bird/tall-tail design, Chuckleroot a broad head and elongated bill, and Mistleaf a taller botanical body with eucalyptus-inspired foliage. Three fictional stages add geometry and change proportions. No GLB, remote texture, HDRI or model service is needed. SVG artwork remains the explicit fallback and grid art.

Accept consent with Demo Explorer selected, then open Map. Inspect an observation/timeline, scan the bee sample, save a record and find it in Contributions and the researcher dashboard. Inspect Bumbloom in 3D, feed Growth XP and visit its Meadow. Community uses curated mock profiles with no private scientific data. The later population pass is recorded in `docs/POPULATION_NOTES.md`.

In Research Projects, create a study and a task. Define category, notes, effort, site and repeat-visit requirements. Publish it, participate through Home → Tasks, then inspect linked records, uncertainty/statuses, export the example dataset and open Site Monitoring. Game rewards never verify records.

Identification, researcher identity, expert review and transmission remain simulated. No live Community publishing/trading, authentication, secure backend or verified guardian pathway is implemented. The default map is an offline schematic; optional attributed OpenStreetMap tiles require explicit activation and internet. Physical iPhone Safari, hardware permissions and mobile GPU performance remain manual checks. HTTPS hosting is required for supported camera/geolocation and Home Screen behaviour.
